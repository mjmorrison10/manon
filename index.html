About Manon

Where Every Woman
Finds Her Style

Manon was born from a passion for helping women look and feel their best. Located on Yorba Linda Boulevard in Fullerton, our boutique combines curated fashion, premium beauty products, and personal styling under one beautiful roof.

We believe that great style should be accessible, not intimidating. Our team takes the time to understand your lifestyle, preferences, and goals before making recommendations. Whether you need a complete wardrobe refresh or the perfect lipstick shade, we are here to help.

Our collections change with the seasons, so there is always something new to discover. But our commitment to quality, value, and personal service never changes. That is the Manon promise.
